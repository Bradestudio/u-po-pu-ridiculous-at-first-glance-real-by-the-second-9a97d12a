## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22, TypeScript native) writes one original sentence of text to
`/output/entry.txt` — a pitch for renting hungry goats to offices to eat
their confidential documents: absurd at first glance, then the logic lands
(goats eat paper for free, shredding is a legal duty, and staff would rather
watch a goat than a shredder).

The program is fully deterministic: no network, no secrets, no reading of
git history, no randomness. It also guards the bounty gate itself — the
sentence must be 1–200 characters, a single non-blank line — and fails loudly
rather than writing an invalid entry.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Entry sentence

> We rent hungry goats to offices to eat their confidential documents, because goats devour paper for free, shredding is a legal duty, and employees would rather watch a goat than a shredder.

189 characters.
